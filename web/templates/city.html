  • Match the specialty to your need — a general dentist for checkups and fillings, an orthodontist for braces or aligners, an oral surgeon for extractions or implants, or an emergency dentist for urgent pain.
  • Read recent reviews — look for consistent comments on bedside manner, wait times and billing, not just the star average.
